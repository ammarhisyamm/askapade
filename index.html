Motion

  • Fast by default: 160ms taps, 240ms state, 420ms sheets. Nothing over 600ms except celebrations.
  • Spring for things that arrive (pins, badges, money). Ease-out for things that move (sheets).
  • One speed cue per screen: hourglass, depleting bar or ring. Never stack them.
  • Haptics: light on chip/tick, success on match/paid, warning on <10 min.
